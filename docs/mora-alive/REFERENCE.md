# MÔRA — reference fidelity contract

The character sheet supplied by Marius on 2026-10-25 is the binding visual target, not inspiration for a new mascot. V01 was rejected. V02 is a work in progress until comparison of actual rendered frames against this sheet demonstrates fidelity.

## Required form
- One rounded, cheeked flame head, continuously rising into a long curved light crest. No separate egg, hat or hair. The crest sweeps left before its narrow upper tip points up/right.
- Small leaf-shaped torso, pinched below the face, flowing down/left into translucent tapered filaments. No cylindrical metal tail or humanoid limbs.
- Tiny curved closed eyes and subtle smile in the reference idle pose. Dark green open eyes for attention; highlights remain small. Face placement must follow the rounded front surface.
- Separate deep emerald polished jade sphere with natural branching veins and a thin gold rim, at head/shoulder height on the right in the reference pose.
- Fine elliptical gold light paths, wrapping in front of and behind the body; restrained sparks. Soft light on the surrounding space and landing surface. No opaque circular pedestal.
- Cream light dominates (reference #F7E9CB), gold #E9B967 is an accent, mint #A9DDC4 and jade #2E7D68 belong to the stone/environment. The body is not green or solid metal.

## Validation boundaries
Use the paused idle frame for the primary comparison; then inspect attention/work and dim rest/error states. Capture desktop Chromium and iPhone Chromium/WebKit. A passing canvas test proves rendering, not likeness. Record remaining differences explicitly. The original supplied image is not a rigged mesh or material scan; reconstruction must still be judged against it visually. Do not call this a 1:1 match before acceptance.

The live event adapter is independent of this visual work. A manually selected reference expression remains explicitly marked as preview; it does not represent actual CORE activity.
